MYCAM iOS

Virtual camera for jailbroken iPhones

What it does

Use a selected prerecorded video as the camera source in supported camera and social applications. Choose installed target apps, manage clips, and control playback from the MYCAM Remote overlay.

Requirements

  • iOS 15 or newer
  • A jailbroken iPhone
  • ElleKit or another compatible tweak injection framework
