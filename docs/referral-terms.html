MYCAM referral rewards terms

Version referral-20261014. Programme availability is shown in your account.

Your code and rewards

Sign in and verify your email to obtain your code. Four qualifying successful subscription payments using your code earn one reward: one transferable MYCAM activation key for a calendar month from activation, or a £10 Amazon.co.uk gift card. Each payment adds 25%. This is not a cash withdrawal programme.

Qualifying payments

Only full, verified live subscription payments through supported MYCAM app payment methods count. Qualifying paid renewals retain the attribution recorded before checkout. Clicks, free access, website key purchases, key redemption, Gold orders, ZeusHold purchases and access transfers do not count. A referral code does not change the buyer's price.

Holds and requests

Progress can be pending until its payment hold finishes. The hold shown in your account applies to reward delivery, never subscription activation. You may submit a request at 100%; admin approval and delivery require valid, mature payment funding. One open request is permitted at a time. Extra progress carries forward.

Refunds and review

Refunded, partially refunded, reversed or disputed payments are ineligible. Their progress is reversed; after a delivered reward, future progress offsets the reversal before another reward is available. No automatic cash charge is made. Requests can be cancelled before delivery. Valid reserved progress is returned after cancellation or ordinary rejection.

Promotion and delivery

No self-referrals, false accounts, fraudulent payments, spam or misleading earnings claims. Clearly disclose referral promotions as advertising where required. Admin may suspend abusive accounts with a review/support route. Approved rewards are delivered privately in your account. Amazon rewards are £10 for Amazon.co.uk; confirm this marketplace before requesting. Supply delays do not silently change the selected reward.

Programme earning or new requests may be paused. Existing accrued progress and valid reward obligations are retained for review. Account login remains separate from MYCAM subscription access.
