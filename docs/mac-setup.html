Install and start your camera

  1. Open the downloaded disk image and drag MYCAM Mac into Applications.
  2. Open MYCAM Mac from Applications. Sign in or create an account, then verify your email.
  3. Activate your existing iOS key. If you need a key, open MYCAM PRO inside the Mac app. Its payment options issue iOS keys only.
  4. Select your image or video, then click Start camera.
  5. Follow the camera-extension setup. On macOS 15 or later, enable MYCAM in System Settings → General → Login Items & Extensions → Camera Extensions. Earlier macOS versions use the system-extension approval guidance in Privacy & Security.
  6. Select MYCAM Mac in your receiving app’s camera list.

Activation and purchases

An iOS key activates one manager at a time. Moving it to this Mac transfers its remaining access from the previous device; it does not restart expiry. You do not need to buy another key simply because you use a Mac. Prices and available payment providers come from the existing iOS payment service.

After paying, return to MYCAM PRO and choose My purchases / Check payment. Activate the confirmed key using the transfer confirmation. Do not pay again while verification is pending.

If an app does not list the camera

Allow camera access in the receiving app. If its list remains stale after an extension update, use MYCAM’s Reopen test browser, Reopen WhatsApp or Reopen Telegram menu action. MYCAM asks before reopening another app.

This release

Camera delivery has been tested on an Intel Mac with macOS 15.8.2, including Safari and WhatsApp desktop. The universal binary includes Apple silicon support; other hardware, macOS versions and receiving apps still need broader testing. Neural Photo is not included in this release. Photo animation, 3D and video/image camera sources remain available.

An internet connection is required for account and iOS-key verification. Mac maintenance or required-update settings can temporarily block access; follow the message shown in the app.
